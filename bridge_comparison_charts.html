🎯 Key Takeaways

✅ Escrow Bridge Advantages

  • Operational in 8 weeks (already 50% done)
  • Lower cash expenses ($6k-$26k vs $54k)
  • Simpler operations (no 24/7 validators)
  • Proven model (WBTC: $11B TVL, 6 years, zero hacks)

⚠️ Escrow Bridge Trade-offs

  • Requires $50k-$500k in LP capital (locked but recoverable)
  • Transfer limits constrained by LP depth
  • Minimal option skips formal audit (higher risk)

✅ Hyperlane Advantages

  • No LP capital required (eliminates liquidity risk)
  • Better scalability at high volumes
  • General message passing (not just tokens)
  • Future-proof for multi-chain expansion

⚠️ Hyperlane Trade-offs

  • 20+ weeks to operational (not live in first 12 weeks)
  • Higher cash outlay ($54k vs $6k-$26k)
  • Higher operational complexity (validators, relayers)
  • Pioneering Gorbagana SVM integration (unknown timeline risk)
Recommendation: If you can secure LP capital, launch with Escrow bridge (Week 8), gather real usage data for 6 months, then decide whether to stay with Escrow, migrate to Hyperlane, or run both based on actual needs.
